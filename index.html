Where it began

Masks, filters, metro platforms and a lot of nerves. We had no idea we were starting the best thing either of us would ever do.

Everyday us

Movie nights, coffee in hand, a cozy room and a heart made with my fingers because I run out of words. The ordinary days are my favourites.

Dinners under the red umbrella

Cheek kisses, silly roars, squished cheeks and the biggest smiles. You make every table feel like the best seat in the house.

Rain, hills and borrowed raincoats

Wet hair, neon ponchos, waterfalls, a very dramatic helmet and the greenest hills. Somehow the soggiest trip was the sunniest one.

What I want to tell you

Five years ago I didn't know that "us" would become my favourite word. Somewhere between the first awkward hellos and now, you became my home, my best friend, and the person I most want to tell everything to.

You've seen me at my goofiest and my grumpiest, and you stayed. You laugh at my jokes (even the bad ones), you pull faces right back at me, and you make ordinary days feel like holidays.

  • You turn every plan, even a rainy one, into a memory.
  • Your smile fixes my worst days without trying.
  • You're my calm, and my chaos, in the best proportions.
  • I'm still excited to see you, five years in.

Thank you for choosing me, again and again. Here's to more mountains, more dinners, more terrible selfie faces, and a lifetime of us.

I love you. Happy 5 years, my love. ♥
